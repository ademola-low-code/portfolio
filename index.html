How I Work

Principles behind every system I ship

01

Nodes are plumbing. The prompt is the product.

Anyone can connect boxes. The thinking that goes into an agent's instructions is what decides whether it behaves properly with a real customer.

02

Map it before I build it.

I work out the flow, the edge cases and the failure states before the first node goes on the canvas. Rebuilding halfway costs you more than planning.

03

Failure is part of the design.

Retries, validation and fallbacks are built in from the start, so the system fails loudly and safely instead of quietly dropping an order.

04

It ships live, not on a slide.

Every build goes into production on the channels your customers already use — WhatsApp, email, your CRM — not a demo that never leaves my screen.

05

Built to hand over.

Clear naming, documentation and a walkthrough, so your team can run and extend the system long after the project ends.

06

Fast, without cutting corners.

I've delivered a full production agent in under 24 hours. Speed comes from preparation and reusable patterns, never from skipping the testing.
