A lead submits a form on your website at 11:15 PM. They are interested, they have budget, and they want to talk. 

By the time your sales team opens their laptops at 9:00 AM the next morning, that lead has already filled in three other forms. One of your competitors replied instantly. You are now chasing a cold email thread.

The standard fix is sending an automated email that says, "Thanks, we received your message and will get back to you within 24 hours." That email does not secure the lead. It tells them to keep looking. 

You do not need a 24/7 human sales team to fix this. You need a simple pipeline that receives the lead, uses an AI model to evaluate if they are a good fit, and either books a meeting directly onto your calendar or extracts their key details so your team can act first thing in the morning.

Here is how to build an after-hours lead qualification system using n8n, OpenAI or Claude, and your CRM.

## The basic architecture

The system needs to do four things between 6:00 PM and 8:00 AM:

1. Catch the incoming lead from your web form or webhook.
2. Filter the enquiry through an AI model with a strict qualification prompt.
3. If qualified: generate a personalized reply with a dynamic scheduling link.
4. Push the structured data and qualification notes directly into your CRM.

Here is the exact n8n workflow structure to set this up.

```
[Webhook / Form Trigger] 
       │
       ▼
[Date & Time Node] ──► (Check if within business hours)
       │
       ├─► (Yes) ──► Standard CRM Routing
       │
       └─► (No)  ──► [AI Agent / LLM Chain]
                           │
                           ▼
                     [Switch Node] (Qualified?)
                      ├── Yes ──► [Send Email w/ Cal.com link] ──► [Create CRM Lead]
                      └── No  ──► [Send Standard Auto-Responder] ──► [Log to CRM]
```

## Step 1: Detect after-hours enquiries

You only want the automated AI responder running when your office is closed. If a lead arrives at 2:00 PM, a human should take it.

In n8n, add a **Date & Time** node right after your form trigger. Set the action to *Format a date*. Use the custom format `HH` to get the hour of the day in your local timezone.

Add an **If** node to check the output:

- **Condition**: Value is greater than or equal to `18` (6 PM) OR less than `8` (8 AM).
- If true, route to the After-Hours AI branch.
- If false, run your standard daytime workflow.

If you handle weekend leads, add a check for the day of the week using the format `ddd`. If the output is `Sat` or `Sun`, route straight to the after-hours pipeline.

## Step 2: Qualify the enquiry with an AI node

Passing raw form entries to an auto-responder leads to generic, robotic emails. Instead, pass the form payload to an **AI Agent** or **Chain LLM** node in n8n. 

Use a fast, reliable model like `gpt-4o-mini` or `claude-3-5-haiku`. The prompt must force the model to return structured JSON, not conversational prose.

Here is a system prompt that works consistently:

```text
You are an expert sales assistant for [Company Name]. Your task is to analyze after-hours inbound leads and decide if they meet our ideal customer profile (ICP).

ICP Criteria:
- Must have an active budget above $2,000.
- Needs service implementation within 30 days.
- Belongs to B2B, SaaS, or E-commerce sectors.

Analyze the user's submission:
Name: {{ $json.name }}
Email: {{ $json.email }}
Company: {{ $json.company }}
Message: {{ $json.message }}

Return a JSON object with these exact keys:
{
  "is_qualified": boolean,
  "reason": "short string explaining why",
  "urgency": "high" | "medium" | "low",
  "personalized_summary": "1 sentence summarizing their core problem"
}
```

By forcing JSON output, the rest of your n8n workflow can make programmatic decisions based on the `is_qualified` field.

## Step 3: Handle qualified leads immediately

If `is_qualified` is `true`, your workflow should attempt to secure a meeting on the spot.

Send an email using the Gmail or Outlook node in n8n. Do not send a stock template. Use the `personalized_summary` generated by the AI model to show you understood their submission.

Include a personalized scheduling link (such as Cal.com or Calendly). If you use Cal.com, you can pre-fill their details in the URL query parameters so the prospect does not have to re-type their name and email:

`https://cal.com/your-team/30min?name={{ $json.name }}&email={{ $json.email }}`

When I built ELARA, an automated spa concierge on WhatsApp, we used live capacity checks to prevent overbooking while capturing late-night interest. The principle is identical for B2B leads: validate availability immediately while the buyer's intent is highest.

## Step 4: Update your CRM with explicit tags

Whether the lead is qualified or not, the data must reach your CRM before morning.

Add your CRM node (HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Salesforce) to the end of both branches. Create the contact record and set these specific fields:

- **Lead Source**: Webform (After-Hours)
- **AI Qualification Status**: Qualified / Unqualified
- **AI Summary**: Insert the `reason` and `personalized_summary` fields.
- **Priority**: Set to High if `urgency` equals "high".

When your sales reps log in at 9:00 AM, they do not need to read through long, ambiguous contact forms. They open their view of "Unbooked After-Hours Leads", read the 1-sentence AI summary, and pick up the phone.

## What usually goes wrong

Building the workflow is straightforward. Making it reliable in production requires handling edge cases that cause automated systems to fail.

### 1. Hallucinated lead qualifications
If a prospect writes "I have no budget right now" in the message box, loose AI prompts might still qualify them because the rest of the text looks polite. 

**The fix**: Explicitly tell the model in the prompt: *"If the user explicitly states they have zero budget or are looking for free work, set is_qualified to false regardless of other factors."*

### 2. Double-messaging existing clients
An existing customer might submit a support request through your sales form at 10:00 PM. An automated sales pitch offering them a discovery call will alienate them.

**The fix**: Before sending the input to the AI node, query your CRM by email address. If the contact exists and has an active "Customer" status, branch off to a support notification path instead of the sales path.

### 3. Out-of-memory errors on long form submissions
Occasionally, a user will paste a 3,000-word document into your contact form. Large payloads can spike processing costs or hit context limits on small models.

**The fix**: Add an **Code** node before your AI node to truncate the input text:

```javascript
// Truncate message field to a max of 1000 characters
const maxChars = 1000;
for (const item of $input.all()) {
  if (item.json.message && item.json.message.length > maxChars) {
    item.json.message = item.json.message.substring(0, maxChars) + '... [truncated]';
  }
}
return $input.all();
```

## Where this goes next

A basic after-hours pipeline handles email forms cleanly. If a large portion of your night-time traffic comes through messaging channels like WhatsApp, Telegram, or SMS, the email-based approach is often too slow.

When building PE-RAS (a medical clinic system running automated reminders across email, SMS, and WhatsApp), we found that patients and leads on messaging apps expect responses within seconds, not minutes. 

You can adapt this exact n8n logic to messaging APIs using Twilio or WhatsApp Business nodes. The core pattern remains the same: trigger on receipt, filter for time, run strict AI qualification, update the CRM, and deliver a dynamic booking link.
